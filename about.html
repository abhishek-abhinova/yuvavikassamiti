About Yuva Vikas Samiti

Empowering communities through sustainable development since 2007

Who We Are

A dedicated NGO working towards social transformation and community empowerment

🎯

Our Mission

To empower marginalized communities through sustainable development programs focusing on health, education, livelihood, and social justice while promoting gender equality and environmental conservation.

👁️

Our Vision

A society where every individual, regardless of gender, caste, or economic status, has equal opportunities for growth, dignity, and sustainable livelihood in a healthy environment.

⚖️

Our Values

Transparency, accountability, inclusivity, sustainability, and community participation guide all our initiatives and programs for maximum social impact.

Our Journey

18+ years of dedicated service to communities

2007

Foundation

Yuva Vikas Samiti was registered as a society under Act 21, 1860 with registration number 831/2007-2008, beginning our journey of community service.

2010

First Major Project

Launched our first major health and education initiative, reaching over 1,000 families in rural Uttar Pradesh.

2015

Expansion

Extended operations to Jharkhand and increased our focus on women empowerment and skill development programs.

2020

Jal Jeevan Mission

Became Implementation Support Agency for Jal Jeevan Mission in Ambedkar Nagar and Balrampur districts.

2025

Present Day

Operating across 11 districts with 50+ active projects, impacting over 10,000 lives through various development programs.
